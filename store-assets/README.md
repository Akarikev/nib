- **Full description** *(max 4000 chars)*:
  ```text
  In a world where AI writes code in seconds, Nib is a quiet little simulation built to remember how coding used to feel.

  Remember the late-night focus? The soft glow of a desk lamp, hunting down a missing semicolon, watching your terminal logs scroll past, sipping warm coffee, and the quiet pride of shipping something small—one keystroke at a time?

  Nib brings that feeling back. Settle into an illustrated coding studio, pick your developer mascot, personalize your terminal with a nickname, and build one small project at a time—from a 15-second "Hello, world" and lo-fi beat scripts to indie games and a tiny OS from scratch.

  FEATURES
  • Nostalgic Coding Simulation: Choose from 8 handcrafted projects (15 seconds to 60 minutes) that keep progressing offline while you live your day.
  • Live Interactive Terminal & Monitor: Watch your developer's computer cycle through a code editor, terminal, preview studio, and cozy wallpaper—or open the interactive console to follow live project logs and run quick commands.
  • Choose Your Developer Mascot: Pick from 4 cozy male and female developer styles and personalize your terminal with an optional nickname.
  • Adopt & Pet a Desk Cat: Save up earned coins to adopt a sleepy ginger friend who naps beside your coffee mug—tap to pet them anytime.
  • Craft Your Dream Setup: Equip a clicky keyboard, cozy headphones, dream monitor, and espresso maker to boost earnings, and decorate with fairy lights, a warm desk lamp, a windowsill plant, a peachy rug, and a moon print.
  • Sunny, Rainy & Moonlit Moods: Switch the window weather anytime for free.
  • Coffee Energy, Ranks & Streaks: Coffee refills every 30 minutes offline to trim 25% off a project timer. Climb developer ranks, build a daily coding streak, and claim milestone coin jars.
  • 100% Offline & Private: No accounts, no servers, no ads, no AI hype, and no in-app purchases. Just you, a quiet room, and the craft of coding.
  ```
